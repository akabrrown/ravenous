**How to Use This Checklist**
=============================

This is the single collection point for every piece of real information the platform needs that isn't a design or engineering decision — phone numbers, addresses, service descriptions, past-event media, pricing, and account access. Nothing in the PRD, TDD, Design Brief, App Flow, or Backend Schema depends on this document to start build — per the project's standard practice, build proceeds with clearly flagged placeholders for anything not yet supplied, and those placeholders are swapped for real content as sections of this checklist come back completed.  
**_Note:_** _Items marked (Required for launch) block going live if left blank. Items marked (Needed later) can follow after V1 launch without delaying it — see PRD §4 for the phase each feature belongs to._

**8\. FAQs (Needed later)**
===========================

Common questions worth pre-loading, grouped by topic — confirm or edit:

*   Booking: How far in advance should I book? Do you travel outside Accra? What happens if the event date changes?
*   Payments: What deposit is required? What payment methods do you accept? Is the deposit refundable?
*   Delivery: How long until we receive our photos/videos? In what format?
*   Equipment: Do you provide your own generator/backup power? What happens if equipment fails during an event?
